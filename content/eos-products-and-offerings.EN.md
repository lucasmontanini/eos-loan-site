## Who Eos is

Eos is an energy and credit group headquartered in Spring, Texas (Greater Houston), founded in 2023 and operated by **Eos Solar, Inc.**, a licensed lender holding **NMLS #2744537**. The group operates under two complementary brands:

- **Eos Backup and Battery** (eos-e.com): sale and installation of residential and commercial battery systems.
- **Eos Loan** (eosloan.com): direct financing for essential projects, under the line *"Credit for what's essential."*

Eos's central differentiator is **vertical integration**: the same group that sells the equipment and coordinates its installation also finances the project with its own capital and proprietary technology. No middlemen, no dealer fee, no surprises in the contract. That translates into faster approvals, more competitive terms, and a single team accountable to the customer from start to finish.

Conceptually, Eos is organized today around **three products**:

1. **Battery & Home Backup**: sale and installation of battery systems.
2. **Consumer Financing**: unsecured personal credit, offered directly by Eos to its own customers or to customers originated by partners.
3. **Commercial Financing**: a commercial credit offering, still at an early stage of expansion.

## 1. Battery & Home Backup

> *"Never Lose Power. Even During Outages."*

### The value proposition

Texas is the U.S. state most affected by power outages: more than 90 grid interruption events per year, with episodes such as Winter Storm Uri (2021) and Hurricane Beryl (2024) leaving millions of homes in the dark for days. What Eos sells is peace of mind: a battery system that takes over the home automatically in under 20 milliseconds when the grid fails, without the family noticing the transition.

Unlike a generator, the system is **silent** (below 25 dB), emits no fumes or carbon monoxide, requires no fuel, needs no scheduled maintenance, and creates no friction with homeowners associations (HOA). And unlike the subscription models common in the market, with Eos **the customer owns the equipment**: "Own it. Don't rent it."

### The platform: Sigenergy SigenStor

Eos standardized its offering on Sigenergy, the platform that won the company's internal evaluation on surge capacity, modularity, 10-year total cost and feature set. The components:

- **Battery module**: 9 kWh nominal (8.76 kWh usable) per module, LFP chemistry, stackable architecture.
- **Smart Controller**: 11.5 kW continuous and 17.1 kW surge per controller, with an integrated hybrid inverter (managing battery, grid and solar input in a single unit).
- **Smart Panel / LoadHub**: an integrated smart panel with circuit management and load shedding, with no separate gateway required.
- **V2X module (25 kW DC bidirectional charger)**: turns the customer's electric vehicle into a backup battery. A typical EV carries 60 to 100 kWh — more than Eos's largest fixed plan. Compatible with dozens of models (Tesla, Ford F-150 Lightning, Rivian, Kia, VW and others), with CCS1 or NACS connectors.
- **Monitoring app**: remote tracking, outage notifications and remote diagnostics.

Each stack pairs one controller with up to 6 modules (54 kWh per stack, or 45 kWh with V2X), and multiple stacks can be connected. All primary components (battery, controller and panel) carry a **10-year warranty**, with a contractual minimum retention of 70% of capacity at the end of the period.

### What Eos sells

The offering is flexible and spans everything from standalone equipment to the complete project:

- A single battery, or systems with multiple modules.
- Controller / load hub.
- V2X module and EV charging infrastructure.
- **Equipment only**, with delivery also available **in states beyond Texas**.
- **Equipment plus full installation**, primarily in the Houston area and the other Texas metros (Austin, Dallas, Fort Worth, San Antonio), with expedited scheduling in Greater Houston.

Residential systems range from 9 to 45 kWh (6 to 30 hours of runtime on essential loads). The commercial line reaches 135 kWh, sized to replace 50 to 100 kW diesel standby generators.

### End-to-end service

When the customer contracts the complete project, Eos handles everything:

1. **A free, no-obligation assessment**, with a 2-minute digital planner and a remote site survey by photo.
2. **Sizing and system design** performed in-house.
3. **Full permitting**: Eos coordinates municipal permits and utility interconnection (typically 2 to 4 weeks).
4. **Installation in 1 to 2 days** by licensed and insured local contractors coordinated by Eos.
5. **Commissioning, inspection and ongoing monitoring**, with an annual inspection included in the service plans.

Total time from contract to energization is typically 3 to 6 weeks.

### In-house financing for battery projects

Where the customer and the state are eligible, Eos finances its own battery projects through the group's credit structure. Residential plans start at approximately **$87 per month** on longer terms, with options ranging from 6 months interest-free up to 240 months. Checking eligibility **does not affect the credit score**, there is no prepayment penalty, and the customer may always choose to pay in full and own the system outright from day one.

## 2. Consumer Financing (Eos Loan)

> *"Credit for what's essential."*

### Eos is the lender, not a marketplace

Eos Loan is a **direct lender**: it originates, approves and funds the loans with its own capital and proprietary technology, under NMLS #2744537. The customer and the partner deal with a single team, from application to funding. No auctioning of applications among banks, no intermediaries adding cost.

### The product: unsecured personal credit — the home is never collateral

The core product is the **unsecured personal loan**: personal credit that **does not use the customer's home as collateral**. Eos does not offer HEA, HELOC, home equity loans or any structure based on the equity in the property. The customer finances the project without putting the home at risk and without the timelines and costs of a mortgage transaction.

Commercial terms vary by partner and by the product enrolled, including APR, term and approval criteria. As general reference:

- **APR around 10%**, varying by product and partner.
- **Flexible terms**, reaching options of 6 to 240 months in categories such as energy storage.
- **No dealer fee**: many financing programs deduct a fee from the amount passed through to the partner, making the project more expensive. Eos does not charge one.
- **No prepayment penalty** and no hidden fees.
- **A credit decision in minutes**, subject to approval, in a fully digital process.
- **Average funding within 48 hours** of approval.
- **An initial check with no score impact** (soft pull), and underwriting that accepts **SSN or ITIN**.

### The partner model

Eos receives applications originated by partners at the point of sale and **finances those partners' customers directly**. One example is **My Pure Filter**, whose water filtration customers are financed by Eos.

For the partner — installer, contractor or reseller — the value is direct:

- The customer applies in minutes through a digital form, at the moment of sale.
- The decision comes back in minutes, and the partner closes more business with a clear monthly payment on the table.
- The customer is the borrower; **the partner assumes no credit risk** and keeps the full margin, with no dealer fee.
- Simple onboarding: application, approval and one training session.
- The partner gets a dashboard to follow every application in progress and to generate an updated link so the customer can resume where they left off.

### Financeable categories

Financing is available across a range of essential product and service categories, among them:

- Batteries and energy storage
- Water filtration (whole-home and point-of-use)
- Roofing
- HVAC
- Home improvement and home repair
- Energy efficiency
- Other eligible products and services offered by partners

Availability of any given category depends on the partner and the product enrolled. **Solar energy is not among the current categories** and is not a vertical Eos intends to prioritize at this time.

## 3. Commercial Financing

Eos also maintains a **commercial credit** line, financing projects for companies in the same essential verticals: commercial energy storage, EV charging infrastructure and commercial-scale water filtration. The rationale mirrors the consumer product: a direct lender, no dealer fee, a fast decision and flexible terms.

Positioning matters here: commercial financing is still a **less developed and less expanded** operation than consumer financing. It should be treated as a secondary, complementary product — not as one of Eos's principal offerings today.

## Why Eos

- **In batteries — singular vertical integration**: equipment, coordinated installation and credit under one group. Less friction, less cost, more speed.
- **In batteries — ownership, not subscription**: the customer owns the system, which appreciates as an improvement to the property.
- **In financing — transparency as a principle**: "The fine print, out loud." No hidden fees, no promises of guaranteed approval, terms always stated plainly.
- **Reputation**: a 4.7-star rating on Google.
- **Brazilian audience**: service in English and Portuguese, from a company that understands what it is like to be a Brazilian borrowing in the United States.
