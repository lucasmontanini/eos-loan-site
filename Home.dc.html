What does Eos Loan finance? +

Essential projects for businesses and people: real estate purchase and renovation, vehicles, equipment, working capital, receivables advance, and home upgrades like roofing, HVAC, windows, water filtration, batteries and solar.

Who can apply? +

US businesses and individuals. You can apply with a Social Security Number or an ITIN — we built our underwriting for both.

Does checking my rate affect my credit score? +

No. Checking your rate uses a soft credit inquiry, which does not affect your score. A hard inquiry only happens if you proceed with a loan.

Can I use property outside the US as collateral? +

Yes — for real estate credit, Eos can accept property in Brazil, Mexico, Colombia, Argentina, Peru, Ecuador, Paraguay and Chile as collateral, subject to valuation and credit approval.

How fast is funding? +

Decisions typically take minutes. After approval, average funding turnaround is 48 hours.

Can my company offer Eos financing to its customers? +

Yes — that's our Solutions line. Add a credit button to your platform, send financing links, or embed cross-border payments, with your brand in front and Eos underwriting behind. Partner with Eos →
